AIE-PL hybrid: one-event latency levers on the AI Engine side (2026-09-12)
==========================================================================
Where the 355 us went (embedding-on-array image, aie_hybrid_pipe_embed):
  ~290 us AIE kernel chain: 6 attention blocks, each a serial chain
         pre -> head post -> a_proj -> b1 -> b2 -> c (65 us per obj/cross block)
  ~30 us  fabric stages at 100 MHz (pairwise 6.8, candidate build 9.2, AE 7.3, lorentz 2.9)
  ~25 us  host launch (ERT scheduler; direct registers remove it)
The interval is the SLOWEST kernel; the latency is the SUM along the chain.

  writeincr (~18 cycles per int16) plus a 2,700-cycle byte-wise memset of the
  "= {0}" stack arrays; gemm and the vector layer norm were already cheap.
  aiesim, object block, us/event: head post 11.7->10.1, post_c 14.3->6.0,

Lever 4 -- the whole ABC stack on the array (aie_chain_graph.h, chain_kernels, commits
06177aa..effa3f8). The six attention subgraphs hand their windows to each other on the
array; small tile kernels do the mask row, remask and candidate build (bit-exact with the
fabric's ap_fixed<16,7> wrap arithmetic). Fan-outs to a block's 4 heads + residual are
kernel STREAMS multicast by the switch (connect<stream, window<>>), as a PLIO does --
plain multi-consumer windows mis-delivered on the ISA model, one window per consumer,
copy trees, and even two one-layer halves in one graph failed the AIE placer. 8 PLIOs
instead of 20; the fabric keeps read/fork, embedding and wij sends, lorentz, AE, write.
aiesimulator, whole stack: x after cross L1 at 111.2 us from t=0, 8.7 us/event; == x86.

ROW STREAMING BETWEEN THE POST KERNELS (POST_STREAM, image aie_hybrid_chain rebuilt
2026-09-14, md5 0c0e0d25, BOOT.BIN.pstream on the FAT, deployed 10:19):
  a_proj -> b1 -> b2 -> c hand over one 16-word row at a time on core streams
  instead of a whole window, so the next kernel starts on row 0 while the previous
  one is on row 1. a_proj keeps its five window inputs; c takes the FFN stream and
  the projection stream (the residual). Block outputs stay stream-multicast to the
  consumers' windows, so the blocks themselves are still serial.
  MEASURED: one event 134 -> 108 us, 114 -> 87 us with direct-register launch;
  interval 8.4 -> 9.5 us (105k events/s, the streams make each kernel wait on its
  producer row by row). Scores identical 2000/2000 vs the chain image, AUC 0.9825.
  aiesimulator predicted it: whole stack 111 -> 77 us, object block 24.2 -> 14.9 us.
  x86sim bit-identical per block (20 events x 6 blocks) and on the chain; the ISA
  model matches x86sim line for line.
  GOTCHA: the port types come from the kernel sources, so every compile of the
  kernels needs --Xpreproc=-DPOST_STREAM (the graph header's #define is not enough;
  without it the graph fails with "adf::stream does not match port type window").

Summary, one event: 355 -> 108 us (3.3x), 87 us direct (4.1x); interval 17.3 -> 9.5 us
(1.8x; the best interval was 8.4 with whole-window handoffs). Fabric-only two-layer
(t2i): 127-136 us / 113 direct, 24.6 us/event. The hybrid now leads on one event by
about 1.25x and on throughput by 2.6x.

2026-09-14 second round of levers (row streaming was the first):
  (a) vector head gather in a_proj: the four head windows were read one word at
      a time (~18 cycles each, 16 a row). The smallest 16-bit vector is 8 lanes,
      so two rows per head are read at once and split with two 4-lane zips.
      x86 BIT-IDENTICAL on all six blocks.
  (b) float-vector layer norm (layernorm_vec.h, LN_INT reverts): the integer one
      is exact to half an LSB but a dependent chain of three reductions and a
      dozen scalar steps (~135 cycles a row) with nothing to hide it behind once
      rows stream. The float version is 8-lane fp32 with a bit-trick rsqrt and
      two Newton steps. Per-block golden errors UNCHANGED (obj 0.034, cross
      0.016), but on the whole chain ONE event of 20 flips a jet's argmax
      category in chain_post_obj (c error 0.55 vs 0.5 tolerance; the other 19
      events stay at 0.01-0.02). That is a decision boundary, not lost accuracy:
      x max err 0.11 vs 0.07. Validate by AUC on hardware, fall back to LN_INT
      for the object post_c kernels if the AUC moves.
  (c) CHAIN_STREAM: the glue kernels (assemble, post-obj) take x as a row stream
      and pass each row on as soon as it is remasked/biased; embed streams too.
  (d) PRE_STREAM: the head-pre kernels project Q, K and V four rows at a time as
      the rows land; only Q*K^T waits for the last row. Object and cross blocks.
  (e) WIJ_ONE_PORT: the fabric sent the SAME pairwise slice to all four heads.
      One port now feeds all four head-post kernels through the stream switch,
      removing three quarters of that transfer (~4.8 us of fabric time).
  (f) LORENTZ_WIDE: exponential cores per call (II 4 -> 1) and the candidate
      sums as an unrolled ordered chain instead of an II=8 accumulator.
  (g) PLIO_FREQ_MHZ so the interface can be declared above 100 MHz; the hybrid
      fabric has 2.03 ns of slack at 100 MHz (critical path 7.97 ns), so 120 MHz
      closes with margin and 125 does not.
  Fabric-only: the kernel clock has 2.54 ns slack at 100 MHz (path 7.46 ns) ->
  a 125 MHz build (plstream_t2j) is the whole lever, no source change.
