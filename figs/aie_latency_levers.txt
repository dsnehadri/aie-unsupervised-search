AIE-PL hybrid: one-event latency levers on the AI Engine side (2026-09-12)
==========================================================================
Where the 355 us went (embedding-on-array image, aie_hybrid_pipe_embed):
  ~290 us AIE kernel chain: 6 attention blocks, each a serial chain
         pre -> head post -> a_proj -> b1 -> b2 -> c (65 us per obj/cross block)
  ~30 us  fabric stages at 100 MHz (pairwise 6.8, candidate build 9.2, AE 7.3, lorentz 2.9)
  ~25 us  host launch (ERT scheduler; direct registers remove it)
The interval is the SLOWEST kernel; the latency is the SUM along the chain.

Lever 1 -- vector window I/O in every AIE kernel (win_vec.h, commit c0f653b + e2f50c2)
  The aiesimulator profile: ~80% of each post kernel was scalar window_readincr /
  writeincr (~18 cycles per int16) plus a 2,700-cycle byte-wise memset of the
  "= {0}" stack arrays; gemm and the vector layer norm were already cheap.
  aiesim, object block, us/event: head post 11.7->10.1, post_c 14.3->6.0,
  a_proj 11.6->3.4, b1/b2 9.7->3.3, head pre 8.1->2.0; embedding 16.8->7.2.
  x86sim bit-identical to the previous kernels (20 events x 6 blocks + embed);
  aiesim == x86sim line for line (obj, cross, embed).
  NEGATIVE: a vector wij bias path was bit-exact in x86sim and wrong in the last
  row of every event on the ISA (with or without a memory fence); kept scalar.

Lever 4 -- the whole ABC stack on the array (aie_chain_graph.h, chain_kernels, commits
06177aa..effa3f8). The six attention subgraphs hand their windows to each other on the
array; small tile kernels do the mask row, remask and candidate build (bit-exact with the
fabric's ap_fixed<16,7> wrap arithmetic). Fan-outs to a block's 4 heads + residual are
kernel STREAMS multicast by the switch (connect<stream, window<>>), as a PLIO does --
plain multi-consumer windows mis-delivered on the ISA model, one window per consumer,
copy trees, and even two one-layer halves in one graph failed the AIE placer. 8 PLIOs
instead of 20; the fabric keeps read/fork, embedding and wij sends, lorentz, AE, write.
aiesimulator, whole stack: x after cross L1 at 111.2 us from t=0, 8.7 us/event; == x86.
